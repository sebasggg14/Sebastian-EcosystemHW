Ecosystem

First things first, I overscoped SO BADLY. Not only did I have a lot of different ideas I wanted to do (a lot of which I wasn’t even able to finish), but I decided I hated myself and did the ecosystem in 3D. While I do think the 3D works really well– the rotation of the animals really sells it, and I wouldn’t have been able to do that in 2D to the same extent– this probably could’ve been at least a little bit easier if it had been done in 2D. I think what feels the worst is that I did put a lot of time into this project, especially during the beginning. I did sort of fall off during the 2nd week because of midterms, but I at least tried to work on it daily, even if it was just for an hour. I’m proud of what I made, but I really wish I had done some things differently. 

I was inspired by this game on the App Store called Tap Tap Fish, which is an AFK game that I used to play when I was little. When the ecosystem assignment was first pitched, this was one of the things that came to my mind, since the game itself isn’t too far off from being an ecosystem in itself. I think one of my favorite things about this ecosystem is the visuals. I used a lot of post-processing tricks to get the fog and aquatic color, but also had to use 2 different cameras + stacking to achieve the split-lighting between underwater and above water.
